### Technical Notes

**Complexity**: Requires fuzzy matching (titles may change slightly between PLAN versions)

**Options**:
1. Exact title match (simple but fragile)

**Recommendation**: Use beads `external_ref` field to store unique PLAN item identifier (e.g., hash of original title). Match on external_ref before creating.

**Deferred**: Wait for real-world usage to see if duplication is actually a problem. May be acceptable for light integration.
